02 / Engineering approach

How I build.

The principles I try to apply while moving from a promising demo to a dependable system.

01

Make behavior observable.

Use structured logs, traces, evaluation datasets, and explicit failure modes instead of relying on intuition.

02

Prefer reproducible experiments.

Keep configurations, assumptions, baselines, and results visible so improvements can be checked.

03

Design for real constraints.

Think about latency, cost, reliability, security, maintainability, and user experience early.
